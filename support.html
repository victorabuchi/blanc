RETURNS

We have a 14-day return policy. You have 14 days after receiving your item to request a return. You are responsible for any payments due to return or exchange the product.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn and unused, with tags attached, and in its original packaging. Proof of purchase is required.

Frozenholm is not responsible for your returns until they arrive at our warehouse. Any lost returns will not be refunded. Proof of delivery must be provided on all return shipments.

To complete a return, email us at support@frozenholm.com with your order number and reason for return. We will respond within 2 working days with return instructions.

REFUNDS

We will notify you once we have received and inspected your return and let you know if the refund was approved. If approved, you will be automatically refunded to your original payment method within 5-10 working days.

Please note that it may take additional time for your bank or card provider to process and post the refund.

CONTACT

For all support enquiries email us at support@frozenholm.com

We aim to respond to all emails within 2 working days.
